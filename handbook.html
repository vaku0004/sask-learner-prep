What to focus on

  • Chapter 4 covers everything in the sign test.
  • Chapter 3 is the core of the basic test: right of way, passing, turning, lanes, pedestrians, headlights.
  • Chapters 5–6: following distance, hazards, freeways, winter driving and emergencies.
  • Chapters 2, 7 and 8: GDL restrictions, alcohol and drug limits, and what police can require.
