Why it's harder than the real test

  • Every wrong answer is a close call. Begins or ends, left or right, recommended or maximum speed, 5 m or 15 m. You have to know the rule, not recognise it.
  • No guessing by length. Right and wrong answers are written to similar lengths, so the longest option is rarely the right one.
  • Answers are shuffled every time. Each new set starts with questions you haven't seen yet, until you've covered all 333.
  • Checked against the handbook. Every question shows the handbook page it comes from, so you can read the rule in SGI's own words.

About the knowledge test

  • A Class 7 learner's licence requires two knowledge tests: Basic (driving situations and rules of the road) and Sign identification.
  • If you fail one part, you only rewrite the part you failed. You may write only once per day.
  • Passed written results are valid for one year.
  • Minimum age is 16, or 15 in a high school driver education program.
  • Bring several pieces of original ID. Photocopies are not accepted. First-time drivers aged 15–18 need a signed Signature/Guarantor form.
  • You'll also take a vision test. After the knowledge tests you drive as a learner for at least nine months.

Source: Saskatchewan Driver's Handbook 2025–26, pages 20–24. SGI does not publish the number of questions or the pass mark in the handbook; this site uses 30 sign questions, 20 rules questions and an 80% pass mark for practice. Book your test through MySGI or a motor licence issuer.
